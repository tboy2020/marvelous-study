Your notebook

My notes

Type a note, or tap the microphone on your keyboard and just talk — the iPad writes it for you. Then have it read back, or ask for a simple summary.

Photo to text

Snap a page

Take a photo of a page from her book or worksheet. The app reads the words from the picture and turns them into a note she can listen to.

Record a lesson

Record

Record the class, listen back (stop and carry on where you left off), and turn the whole recording into written notes.

Turning a recording into words works best on the laptop (it is too heavy for a phone). First time, it downloads a small language model (about 75MB), then it works offline and runs fast on a strong laptop.

Your tutor

Ask for help

Ask anything about Hospitality and Catering — theory, coursework, or the practical exam. She explains simply and helps you understand it in your own words.

Your NEA / coursework

Coursework helper

Upload your task, or your own draft (a photo or a PDF). I explain what it means, help you plan it, and check your work — but I will never write it for you. Your coursework has to be your own, or it does not count.

Photo, PDF, or Word document (.docx) all work. A clear photo reads most reliably.
