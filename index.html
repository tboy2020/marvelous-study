Set up & make it yours

Settings

Saves ALL her notes to a file you keep. Do this often, and email the file to yourself. If a browser ever clears its data, restore it here and nothing is lost.

This saves her notes, highlights, coursework and recipe documents — everything except the big recording audio (its written words are still saved). When you press it, choose your Downloads or Desktop folder (these are the most reliable — you can copy the file into OneDrive afterwards). Then email the file to yourself to be safe. Recordings will sync fully once she moves to the cloud on her new device.
