Study LabMarvelous's smart study helper

Your notebook

My notes

Type a note, or tap the microphone on your keyboard and just talk — the iPad writes it for you. Then have it read back, or ask for a simple summary.

Photo to text

Snap a page

Take a photo of a page from her book or worksheet. The app reads the words from the picture and turns them into a note she can listen to.

Record a lesson

Record

Record the class, listen back (stop and carry on where you left off), and turn the whole recording into written notes.

Turning a recording into words works best on the iPad or laptop — it is too heavy for a phone. On a phone, use the keyboard microphone in Notes instead. First time, it downloads a small language model (about 40MB), then works offline.

Your tutor

Ask for help

Ask anything about Hospitality and Catering — theory, coursework, or the practical exam. She explains simply and helps you understand it in your own words.
